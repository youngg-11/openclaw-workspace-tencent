# Obsidian 同步

## 路径
- Obsidian仓库: D:\Openclaw\Obsidian仓库
- OpenClaw workspace: d:\Openclaw\data\workspace
- 同步入口: Obsidian仓库/05 Openclaw/workspace

## 命令
- 搜索: `notesmd-cli.exe search "关键词" --vault "D:\Openclaw\Obsidian仓库"`
- 读取: `notesmd-cli.exe print "笔记名" --vault "D:\Openclaw\Obsidian仓库"`
